# Demonstration limits

- All companies, financial figures, prices, holdings and trades are invented for this candidate. The fixture is not a redacted historical portfolio. No displayed return is evidence of actual or backtested strategy performance.
- SPY and QQQ are recognizable comparison-slot labels. Their prices are artificial, share the authored clock and exclude dividends. The daily path uses weekdays, not a full exchange calendar; the 1D view has sparse illustrative observations.
- Input statuses such as `verified_numeric` and quote `closeStatus: verified` are inherited internal consistency states. They do not assert SEC/provider verification. Source digests are explicitly labelled all-zero placeholders. The fixture does not reproduce ingestion, filing parsing, corporate actions or independent metric derivation.
- Price-target records were excluded. The interface shows an unavailable target rather than reusing private saved valuations. Pure target calculators remain for code inspection; their assumptions are not redesigned or validated for investment use here.
- Claims/citation registers, company descriptions and category membership were replaced or emptied. Only fictional company descriptions are shown. Real-company symbols in isolated legacy unit tests are ordinary public identifiers, not demo holdings or saved trades.
- No provider ingestion, Cloudflare/D1 backend, local tracker service, account management, scheduling, owner controls or deployment is included. A successful demo test does not validate the private app's integration/deployment tests. HTTP `/api/*` requests to Vite are not a JSON backend; only browser application fetch calls are handled by the in-memory adapter.
- The interface and engine retain historical policy identifiers and explanatory strings. Their dates identify retained policy revisions, while the demo clock is June 2026. This ordering is illustrative and must not be represented as a historical test of those policies.
- Build output currently has a main-chunk size warning. Type/lint/regression/build checks cover this candidate, not comprehensive accessibility, all browsers or all source-app features. Browser QA details and gaps are in `VALIDATION.md`.
- The dependency inventory comes from the exact lockfile; platform-optional packages are included even when absent on the validation machine. Notices collected from installed packages are a review aid, not a legal opinion or complete binary-distribution compliance bundle. A fresh vulnerability advisory review and rights review remain publication decisions.
- The project has no selected licence. Preparing a local candidate grants no new redistribution rights. Do not publish until the owner confirms original source/asset rights, project licence and the intended publication scope.
